Case Study 01 B2B SaaS · 15-Person Ops Team

Eliminating a 3-Hour Daily Reporting Workflow with Automated Data Fusion

The Challenge

Operations pulled metrics across HubSpot, Stripe, and a PostgreSQL cluster every morning, manually transforming records in Google Sheets for leadership briefing. The routine consumed 15 hours weekly, introduced human error, and delayed critical decision-making.

The ESPADA Solution

Architected an automated n8n execution pipeline triggering daily at 07:00 UTC. It queries APIs concurrently, normalizes data models, syncs directly to structured analytics tables, and posts an executive visual briefing directly to Slack with zero human intervention.
